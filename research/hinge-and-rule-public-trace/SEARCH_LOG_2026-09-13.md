## Evidence rules

1. A self-authored post proves that the claim was publicly made, not that every detail is independently verified.
2. A client/collaborator post is stronger for contribution and use, but reuse requires permission.
3. A platform project page is strong for scope and operating detail when its deep URL is retrievable.
4. A registry-derived directory result is an identity lead until legal spelling and status are confirmed.
5. Search snippets contaminated by adjacent Instagram recommendations are not quoted as target-post content.
6. Search-result absence is never treated as proof that no work or page existed.
