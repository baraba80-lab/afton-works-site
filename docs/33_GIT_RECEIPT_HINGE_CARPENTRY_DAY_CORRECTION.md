## Founder correction

The founder rejected the previous separation of field consultation, planning, drawing and carpentry into multiple standalone services.

The binding model is now:

- Hinge & Rule will not take full-scope interior contracts.
- Hinge & Rule will not offer planning-only or design-only commissions.
- The founder works as a design-capable carpenter paid by the carpentry workday.
- Measurement, consumer consultation, field sketches, CAD, cost and material estimation, order support and direct tool work are all valid work within the same paid day.
- A day used mostly for planning is still one paid carpentry day.
- Workshop furniture is fabricated and delivered; large furniture and structural carpentry may be made on site.
- The client approves, orders and pays for materials directly.
- The client directly selects, contracts, pays and may replace other trades.
- Hinge & Rule may introduce people and translate intent, dimensions and sequence, but does not manage other trades or the whole site.
- No total project budget, hidden trade margin, overall schedule, overall completion promise or third-party defect responsibility passes through Hinge & Rule.
