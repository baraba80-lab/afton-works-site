# Instagram Archive Transition Plan

Date: 2026-09-13  
Status: revised planning strategy; no Instagram mutations performed
Accounts reviewed: `@hinge_and_rule`, `@hinge_and_rule_official`, `@sumboy_v_and_v`

## Decision

Do not erase Hinge & Rule and do not keep presenting it as an expanding conventional full-scope interior contractor.

Close its active era with dignity by converting the account family into a legible record of the founder's method. Use UNSEEN_RATIO as the editorial bridge from that material practice into AFTON's research on scent, memory, image, and perception.

The transition is:

`space / furniture / making → invisible conditions → scent / perception → AFTON`

This is continuity, not a sudden rebrand.

## 2. Hinge & Rule: preservation before curation

The 540-post founder account is not clutter alone. It is evidence of a decade moving through construction, furniture, spatial design, exhibition, brand work, and later system/research work.

### Preserve by default

- completed spaces and objects
- construction and workshop process
- sketches, mockups, samples, and mistakes that reveal method
- collaborator, exhibition, and client context where publication is permitted
- captions that show how use was anticipated
- posts that connect design intent to a physical result

### Archive selectively, not emotionally

- accidental duplicates without additional context
- technically unusable images that communicate nothing
- expired promotion with no archival or project value
- private/sensitive client material
- posts whose rights or credits cannot be established
- unrelated fragments that weaken both the founder record and current direction

Never mass-delete. First record URL, date, project, people/credits, media status, and KEEP / REWRITE / ARCHIVE / RIGHTS-CHECK classification. Archiving is reversible; deletion is not the default.

Publish one nine-post Volume One edition. It should read as a compressed retrospective and transition threshold, not a funeral notice and not a fake relaunch.

Instagram displays newer posts first, so production and publishing order must be calculated from the final grid rather than posted in reading order. Verify the grid with private drafts before any live publication.

### Post structure

Each post is a carousel, not a single decorative tile.

1. restrained cover frame
2. one definitive outcome
3. one construction or material detail
4. one drawing, model, or system artifact
5. one concise project record: year / place / role / collaborators / what changed

The grid covers provide order; the carousels provide proof. Avoid nine typography-only squares.

### Closing statement

The message is not “we failed” or “we are gone.” It is:

> Hinge & Rule was a way of finding the hidden rule behind a space, an object, and the way it would be used. The office is becoming quieter, but the method continues in new forms.

Name AFTON, FORMUTE, or UNSEEN_RATIO only where the connection is real. Do not turn the retrospective into an advertisement for the next project.

## 6. UNSEEN_RATIO publishing model

UNSEEN_RATIO should feel like a publication that sometimes produces scents, not a fragrance shop that borrows art direction.

### Editorial unit: one Issue

Each Issue runs for 5–7 feed posts over 3–4 weeks.

| Sequence | Post type | Function |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | SENSATION | present an unresolved perception before naming it |
| 2 | RECORD | time, place, light, temperature, bodily or spatial observation |
| 3 | EVIDENCE | real material, room, tool, residue, sample, or field photograph |
| 4 | TRANSLATION | show how the observation becomes a visual or scent variable |
| 5 | DOSSIER | reveal the scent name and its structure without exposing protected formula data |
| 6 | OBJECT | show sample, bottle, paper, soap, room, or another actual carrier |
| 7 | RETURN | revisit the first sensation after the object exists |

Not every Issue needs all seven. The sequence is a narrative grammar, not a content quota.

### First proposed Issue

**ISSUE 01 — AFTER BLUE SUNSET**

The issue should begin with the observed aftertone of late light and only later disclose the AFTON name/product relationship.

Candidate evidence:

- four-o'clock or declining natural light
- cold metal and damp paper
- coarse linen, imperfect fold, and shadow overlap
- a real working table and sample evidence
- diffusion, persistence, dryness, and edge softness translated visually
- the first physical test object, when it exists

## 7. Visual and caption system

### Image ratio

- about 70% real evidence: rooms, materials, tools, samples, hands, work states
- about 20% interpretive imagery: generated or manipulated only when it expresses something photography cannot
- about 10% typography / index / identity

This is a direction, not a performance metric. Real evidence prevents the feed from becoming an AI moodboard.

### Visual rules

- natural light, low contrast, warm ivory, mineral grey, grey-green, faded blue
- large quiet fields with one materially specific anchor
- restrained vertical alignment and editorial spacing
- texture from the subject, not aggressive filtering
- one controlled contradiction per Issue
- products appear as evidence, not glamour props

Avoid overt diffuser advertising, café-like promotion, generic luxury serif styling, excessive emotional prose, and rigid three-tile checkerboards.

### Caption record

Each caption can carry five fields without showing them as a database UI:

`record code / time-place / observed sensation / material clue / unresolved question`

Example tone:

> UR-01.03  
> 16:27, light leaving the west wall.  
> The room cooled before the air did.  
> Metal, damp paper, coarse linen.  
> Which part remained after the source disappeared?

Korean should lead when the perception was first formed in Korean. English can follow as a compact translation, not as decorative duplication.

## 8. Sustainable cadence

The speed problem is solved by batching, not by daily improvisation.

- feed: 2 posts per week
- Stories: working evidence when it actually exists; no compulsory daily posting
- one Issue prepared at least 70% before publication begins
- monthly review: keep / deepen / stop decisions
- one owner for final visual/editorial approval

Content production stages:

`CAPTURE → SELECT → WRITE → REVIEW → APPROVE → PUBLISH → RECORD RESULT`

Reuse the existing draft → review → approved → publishing → completed/error queue where available.

## 9. Recovery and measurement

The accounts must return more than likes.

| Asset to recover | Signal |
|---|---|
| authorship | profile discovery, credited references, portfolio/site visits |
| proof | saved posts, shares, project-page completion |
| audience | qualified follows, repeat viewers, replies using sensory language |
| demand | sample requests, collaboration inquiries, waitlist or reservation intent |
| research | recurring language, perception patterns, test participation with consent |
| revenue | paid sample/product conversion, B2B lead, workshop or development inquiry |

Never move personal perception replies into a research dataset without an explicit consent and privacy rule.

## 12. Revised continuation — field design and making

Hinge & Rule will not necessarily become static after the Volume One edition. The founder will stop taking full-scope interior contracts and continue as a design-capable carpenter. Planning, measurement, sketching, CAD, material takeoff, order support and direct carpentry are not separate design products: they are different kinds of work performed within a paid carpentry day. Design-only and planning-only commissions are not offered.

The feed must gradually explain this operating transition before exposing AFTON and AI work:

`VOLUME ONE → WHAT CHANGES → WHAT REMAINS → ONE CARPENTER, TWO LANGUAGES → A DAY IS THE UNIT → MATERIALS IN YOUR HAND → DIRECT TO THE MAKER → HINGE MAKING → AFTON / AI CONTINUATION`

Clients order and pay for materials directly. Clients also select, contract, pay and may replace other trades directly; Hinge & Rule does not manage those trades or the whole site. Do not market this as contract-free day labor, a design consultancy, or general contracting. Use `docs/31_HINGE_FIELD_DESIGN_MAKING_TRANSITION.md` v0.2 as the binding operating and content model.
