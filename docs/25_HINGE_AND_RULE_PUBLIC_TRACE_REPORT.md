# Hinge & Rule Public Trace Report

## 한국어 핵심 판단

이번 추적에서 확인된 핵심은 **힌지앤룰의 성과가 부족한 것이 아니라, 저자성과 기여도가 여러 플랫폼에 흩어져 회수되지 못하고 있다는 점**이다. 2016년부터 이어진 공간·가구·제작 기록, 고객이 직접 남긴 역할 증언, 소푸치노 제품 협업, 숨고의 예산·기간·프로세스 기록을 함께 놓으면 힌지앤룰은 단순 인테리어 계정이 아니라 `현장 관찰 → 개념 설계 → 가구·오브젝트 제작 → 시공 → 실제 사용 검증`을 반복한 통합 설계 실천으로 읽힌다.

따라서 마무리 목표는 계정을 예쁘게 정리하는 데 있지 않다. **12개의 검증된 케이스, 정확한 역할·크레딧·권리, 원본 자료, 외부 증거 링크를 하나의 소유 가능한 아카이브로 결속하는 것**이 먼저다. 이 아카이브는 힌지앤룰의 과거를 보존하는 동시에 UNSEEN_RATIO, FORMUTE, AFTON에서 창업자 역량을 증명하고 현재 사업으로 가치를 회수하는 기반이 된다.

첫 회수 대상은 홍대 운전연습실, 세라워크 스튜디오, 영종도 주거 프로젝트, 소푸치노 디퓨저 트레이, 메종판타지아, 초기 영종 상업공간 1건이다. 제3자 이미지와 협업물은 공개 가시성과 재사용 권리를 구분하고, 허락 전에는 복제·재게시하지 않는다.

## Executive finding

Hinge & Rule already has enough public evidence to support a ten-year practice spanning construction, custom furniture, interior and spatial design, brand application, on-site delivery, and later system-oriented research. The central problem is not absence of work. It is that the evidence is fragmented across self-published posts, client posts, collaborator credits, service-platform records, short-form video, and business directories, while no owned index joins those fragments into a durable authorship record.

The most valuable newly recovered material is not another image from `@hinge_and_rule`. It is third-party evidence: a resident crediting Hinge & Rule for furniture design, Soappuccino identifying Hinge & Rule as the maker of a product collaboration, client records describing on-site process and satisfaction, and Soomgo pages attaching scope, sequence, budget, duration, service terms, and completed outputs to the business.^1 ^2 ^3 ^4

The archive should therefore close as a **verified body of work**, not as an abandoned social feed. Hinge & Rule's final public function is to make the founder's method and contribution searchable, then hand active attention forward to UNSEEN_RATIO, AFTON, and the present R&D system without surrendering the accumulated proof.

## Research boundary

This report records public, observable traces found on 2026-09-13 and the authenticated read-only Instagram surfaces already indexed in this repository. No account, post, profile, message, comment, review, business listing, or third-party page was changed.

The search covered:

- Korean and English brand-name variants;
- the two Instagram accounts and linked Threads identity;
- Instagram posts in which third parties tagged the main account;
- project, client, collaborator, and location terms recovered from the archive;
- service and business directories;
- customer-side home and social records;
- YouTube references and search-index remnants.

Search visibility is not the same as factual proof. Each source in the companion ledger is classified by relationship and confidence. Search snippets that could not be opened at their original deep URL are retained as leads, not treated as conclusive evidence.

## Evidence architecture

The trace divides into four layers. They should not be presented as if they have equal evidentiary weight.

| Layer | What it proves | Strong examples |
|---|---|---|
| Authored record | chronology, intent, vocabulary, process, self-claimed role | 540-post main archive; 131-post portfolio; Threads method notes |
| Client/collaborator record | work existed and another party recognized a contribution | Soappuccino tray credits; residential kitchen and furniture credits; Maison Fantasia references |
| Transaction/operations record | scope, budget, duration, sequence, A/S, service category | Soomgo profile and project pages |
| Registry/discovery record | business name, category, regional existence, search aliases | MoneyPin, Carmap, regional directory results |

The client/collaborator and operations layers are the most useful for recovering value. They turn a private claim into externally corroborated proof.

## Recovered chronology

### 2016–2020 — site, making, and local commercial work

Instagram preserves the main archive back to March 2016. The early record is dominated by site conditions, fabrication, repair, furniture, small commercial environments, and the practical transition from drawings to use. Search-indexed portfolio traces add Yoga Garden, Nail Gonggam, and other Yeongjong commercial work.^9 ^10

This period should not be summarized as “early interiors.” It established the operating grammar that remains visible later:

- observe the real condition before fixing a concept;
- join space, furniture, finish, and signage rather than treating them as separate deliveries;
- design around operating use;
- solve within limited budget and local fabrication conditions;
- remain accountable through the physical result.

A 2020 indexed post explicitly described a rare full-demolition project and noted that many earlier spaces had been transferred to new owners.^11 That sentence is historically important. It anticipates the founder's present concern: built value remained in the place while authorship and upside moved elsewhere.

### 2021–2023 — integrated space, object, and client systems

The public trace becomes more legible as named projects and third-party posts accumulate. `채움소 / 프로젝트섬소년`, `예원피아노학원`, `이얼라운드`, residential kitchens, counters, washstands, and Soappuccino objects show the practice working across scales.^12 ^13 ^14

The strongest residential cluster is a series of client-authored posts from 2022. One post credits an architect for the washstand design and Hinge & Rule for the furniture; others show the completed kitchen and describe the founder as a considerate, highly perceptive collaborator who checked progress through construction.^1 ^15 ^16 These posts prove more than visual taste. Together they establish role separation, client experience, execution, and use after completion.

The Soappuccino collaboration is similarly strong. Two 2023 client posts identify Hinge & Rule as the direct maker of a rough lauan-wood diffuser tray and later credit `@hinge_and_rule × @soappuccino` for a tray used in an actual sales promotion.^2 ^3 This is a compact early precedent for the current AFTON/FORMUTE model: scent, object, presentation, and commerce were already being joined in one deliverable.

### 2024–2026 — formal portfolio evidence and expanded systems

Soomgo adds unusually concrete project data. The Hongdae driving-practice project is recorded at KRW 47 million, one month, with a sequence from concept design to detailed design, field application, and completion.^4 The Cerawork Studio project is recorded at KRW 35 million and one month, and describes layout redesign, workflow optimization, custom furniture design/fabrication/installation, sink and storage work, and symbol renewal.^17

These pages are valuable because they prove that Hinge & Rule was not operating only as a stylist or image-maker. The documented offer combined concept, drawings, fabrication, site delivery, and brand expression.

The 2025–2026 record then widens into cultural collaboration, Maison Fantasia, Soappuccino rebranding, FORMUTE, and system/LLM work. Maison Fantasia is independently visible through the client's YouTube and Instagram surfaces, while Hinge & Rule's own description defines its role as interior concept, furniture design, and making.^18 ^19 The current archive must distinguish what is already verified from what still requires collaborator confirmation, particularly exhibition imagery and work involving other artists.

## Operating method proven by public records

The Soomgo profile describes a concrete sequence: site inspection, preliminary layout and estimate, contract, design fixation, formal estimate, then construction. It states a typical two-week design-fix period followed by approximately one month of site work, plus a twelve-month A/S term subject to consumables and later paid service.^23

This is strategically important. It proves that Hinge & Rule's method was not only conceptual. It had an operating spine:

`inspect → frame the concept and budget → fix the design → price formally → build → support after completion`

The weakness is presentation. The current service-platform language compresses a distinctive integrated practice into ordinary “interior service” categories, and some later portfolio prose reads like generic platform marketing. The owned archive should preserve the actual process, project constraints, and decisions while removing vague claims.

## Search and attribution diagnosis

### Strong assets

1. **Long chronology:** the main account visibly reaches 2016.
2. **Cross-scale evidence:** site repair, construction, furniture, objects, complete spaces, branding, and systems all appear.
3. **Third-party corroboration:** clients and collaborators name Hinge & Rule's contribution.
4. **Operational specificity:** platform pages expose budget, time, process, and A/S.
5. **Regional density:** repeated Yeongjong work creates a believable local network, not a collection of disconnected mockups.
6. **Method continuity:** the same use-first, material-aware method is visible before the current language of venture building and perception systems.

### Search liabilities

1. **Name collision:** English searches are overwhelmed by the global dating app and generic hardware results.
2. **Spelling drift:** `힌지앤룰`, `힌지엔룰`, `힌재앤룰`, `Hinge & Rule`, and account handles fragment retrieval.
3. **Two-account split:** the working archive contains the method, while the official account contains cleaner outcomes; neither links the whole story.
4. **No owned canonical index:** search engines find platform pages, not a founder-controlled work record.
5. **Weak credit schema:** many posts name a place but not year, exact scope, collaborators, photographer, or measurable result.
6. **Rights uncertainty:** tagged posts and exhibition material are visible but not automatically reusable.
7. **Context collapse:** personal records, client work, commentary, and later research occupy the same surface without an index.
8. **Platform dependence:** some results exist only as search snippets or logged-in social surfaces and can disappear or change.

## Strategic interpretation

Hinge & Rule should not be repositioned as a studio seeking endless new interior commissions. Its highest value is as the **material proof layer of the founder's operating system**.

The archive proves that the founder can:

- enter an incomplete physical or commercial condition;
- identify hidden operational constraints;
- turn them into a concept and spatial system;
- design the furniture/object layer;
- work through fabrication and site reality;
- hold the result long enough to test use;
- translate the learned pattern into the next system.

This is precisely the capability AFTON needs. AFTON's site should therefore not borrow an anonymous luxury-fragrance posture. It should inherit Hinge & Rule's evidence structure: field condition, drawing, material junction, prototype, completed use, residual trace, and the system extracted from it.

## Recovery plan

### Phase 1 — preserve before editing

- Export original media and captions for the selected core cases.
- Record every third-party URL, screenshot date, media owner, and permission status.
- Keep the 540-post and 131-post inventories unchanged until the final selection is approved.
- Verify the legal/business spelling before propagating directory data.
- Never copy client media into the public website without permission or a valid embedding/licensing basis.

### Phase 2 — create a twelve-case owned archive

Build twelve canonical case files, not a 540-item dump. Each case must contain:

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Identity | canonical title, year, place, client |
| Contribution | exact founder/studio role |
| Problem | operating or spatial condition |
| System | concept, layout, furniture, material, business logic |
| Making | drawings, fabrication, site decisions |
| Result | completed use, budget/time only when publishable |
| Evidence | original files and third-party links |
| Credits | client, architect, maker, artist, photographer |
| Rights | show/embed/reuse permission status |
| Continuity | what method or asset moved into later work |

The first six should be: Hongdae driving practice, Cerawork Studio, Yeongjong residence, Soappuccino tray, Maison Fantasia, and one early local commercial project. The other six should be chosen only after archive review.

### Phase 3 — publish a canonical Hinge & Rule record

The public page should be small and durable:

- `/hinge-and-rule` — decade overview and founder role;
- `/hinge-and-rule/work` — twelve verified cases;
- `/hinge-and-rule/method` — inspect/design/make/use/retain;
- `/hinge-and-rule/credits` — collaborator and rights index;
- `/hinge-and-rule/continuation` — how the method continues through UNSEEN_RATIO, FORMUTE, and AFTON.

Use structured metadata with the canonical name plus alternate Korean/English spellings. Each case page should link to its source evidence and give the founder an explicit contribution line.

### Phase 4 — close the social archive without erasing it

- Keep `@hinge_and_rule` as the founder archive.
- Use the planned final nine-post edition to explain what remains and what continues.
- Turn `@hinge_and_rule_official` into a pointer to the canonical work index rather than another daily feed.
- Do not bulk-delete or archive posts before the twelve-case evidence set is safely retained.
- Send active research and future product narration to UNSEEN_RATIO/AFTON, while linking back to Hinge & Rule as proof of method.

### Phase 5 — recover value from proof

Every published case must support at least one current function:

- founder credibility for AFTON and FORMUTE;
- a paid design/system offer;
- a partner or manufacturing conversation;
- proof for investment, grant, or R&D review;
- a reusable method, dataset, or design rule;
- search attribution that remains under founder control.

The archive is successful only if it compounds into present work. Nostalgia alone is not a recovery mechanism.
