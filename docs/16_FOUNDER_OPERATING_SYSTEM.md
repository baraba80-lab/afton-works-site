# Founder Operating System

Date: 2026-09-13  
Status: binding strategic context for agents  
Principle: **Do the maximum useful work, move quickly, and recover value deliberately.**

## The old loop

The repeated historic loop was:

`conceive → research → design the business → build → prove → hand over → disappear → restart from zero`

This created real outcomes for clients and collaborators but prevented the founder's reputation, intellectual property, data, distribution, and capital from compounding in one place.

The issue was not generosity, collaboration, or letting the best-suited person lead. The issue was absence of an explicit recovery layer and the loss of momentum between creation and commercialization.

## The new loop

Every project must now complete this loop:

`conceive → document → assign ownership → build → measure → sell → retain evidence/data/IP → compound`

Handoff may happen, but disappearance is no longer the default ending.

## Division by capability, not control

The intent is not to own or personally perform everything.

- The founder leads concept, planning, business structure, design, system architecture, communication, and marketing because those roles match demonstrated working strengths.
- Soappuccino leads perfumery practice, material handling, formula/sample work, sensory evaluation, and product applicability because those roles match its practical expertise.
- Shared research is designed together where sensory practice and data/system structure meet.
- Execution may be delegated when another person is better suited, but scope, expected output, deadline, and recovery terms must remain explicit.

This division has already been discussed in principle with Soappuccino. Future documents should formalize it without rewriting the relationship as adversarial.

## Speed rule

Speed is the current constraint. The operating rule is:

1. do the maximum that can be done with present people, tools, materials, and channels;
2. avoid waiting for the final studio, full equipment stack, or completed AI model;
3. reduce work to the smallest sellable and testable unit;
4. keep decisions reversible until payment, production, or legal commitment;
5. never allow documentation to become a substitute for making, selling, and learning.

## Six retained assets

Every meaningful project should return at least four of these six assets to the participating operating system. The exact owner may differ by contribution and prior agreement. A project that leaves only a fee and no reusable value is contract work, not venture building.

| Asset | Minimum retained form |
|---|---|
| Cash | fee, deposit, margin, royalty, or recurring revenue |
| IP | method, system, code, schema, design rule, formula right, or license |
| Data | research record, sensory feedback, version history, or validated mapping |
| Proof | publishable case study and permission to show contribution |
| Audience | direct channel, subscriber, follower, or identifiable returning customer |
| Distribution | supplier, manufacturing, retail, studio, partner, or repeatable sales path |

## Project acceptance gate

Before starting a new external or collaborative project, record:

1. What is paid now?
2. What reusable asset remains afterward?
3. Who owns the source files, system, dataset, formula, and brand?
4. Can the founder publicly show and accurately credit the work?
5. Does the project create direct customer or distribution access?
6. What is the stop condition if the partner does not execute?

Do not accept an arrangement where all six answers disappear outside the participating system.

## Attribution rule

Public communication must distinguish contribution rather than hide it behind a client or collaborator.

Use explicit credits where applicable:

- Founder / Venture & System Direction
- Brand and Experience Architecture
- Spatial / Furniture / Fabrication Direction
- Scent Data and Perception Research
- Product and Commercialization Design

Credits must match actual work. They are not vanity labels; they preserve discoverability and future deal value.

## Ownership rule

Avoid undefined “joint ownership” as a default because it can make essential assets unusable without later consent. Prefer:

1. one identified owner for each asset;
2. explicit licenses for the other party;
3. defined commercial fields, duration, territory, sublicensing, derivative use, and termination effects;
4. a contribution and version ledger in the repository;
5. written approval before third-party transfer, model training, API exposure, publication, or licensing.

Background IP and newly created project IP must be separated before work begins.

## Repository continuity protocol

This repository is the public-safe coordination record for AFTON.

Each material session must leave:

- a dated decision or research document;
- changed assumptions and rejected directions;
- exact next action and stop gate;
- links or paths to evidence;
- a coherent commit message;
- no credentials, private customer data, formulas, personal financial details, or confidential partner material.

Every Codex/Work agent must read `AGENTS.md`, `CODEX_TASK.md`, and the current cross-agent handoff before acting.

## Recovery rule

“회수” means deliberate recovery of the resources invested in building the work. It is broader than short-term cash and is not a claim to take everything from a collaborator.

For each project, record and recover:

- direct costs and unpaid labor;
- agreed fee, margin, royalty, or revenue share;
- reusable know-how, code, data structure, process, and research evidence according to contribution;
- case-study and credit rights;
- future customer, partner, manufacturing, or distribution access where agreed;
- the right and practical ability for each party to continue using its own background assets.

Recovery terms should be agreed before the project creates large sunk costs. This is a shared operating safeguard for both the founder and Soappuccino.

## Continuous execution cadence

“Non-stop” does not mean doing every workstream simultaneously. It means no gap between validated stages.

Operate one weekly loop:

1. **Build** — produce one real artifact, sample, page, or system improvement.
2. **Evidence** — photograph, measure, tag, and record it.
3. **Offer** — connect it to an actual paid proposition or customer test.
4. **Learn** — capture sensory, operational, and commercial feedback.
5. **Decide** — continue, revise, package, or stop.

The following week begins from retained evidence, not from a new blank page.

## Founder visibility without founder theatrics

The founder does not need to become an influencer or place a portrait on every surface. Visibility can be structural:

- signed decisions and field notes;
- real hands, tools, materials, samples, and prototypes;
- versioned research and measured changes;
- explicit project credits;
- a coherent archive showing how one result led to the next;
- first-person writing only when it adds evidence or judgment.

The goal is not self-promotion. It is preventing authorship and accumulated capability from becoming invisible again.
