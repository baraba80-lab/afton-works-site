# FAMILY A — FIELD / DIFFUSION

**Prompt**
> Contemporary abstract editorial image for an experimental fragrance practice. A precise quiet composition on warm ivory with graphite, muted mineral blue and dusty grey-green. The field should feel physical yet hard to identify, somewhere between paper dust, condensation, pigment and memory. Strong underlying grid logic, large negative space, asymmetrical balance. Include exactly one tiny artificial coral-peach interruption that is slightly displaced from the system. Soft indirect daylight, restrained material texture, museum-catalogue calm. Beautiful but unresolved, familiar but not identifiable. No text, no logo, no perfume bottle, no flower, no liquid CGI blob, no scientific diagram, no cyber interface, no glossy luxury lighting.

# Failure corrections

Use these only as one-variable corrections, not as full prompt replacements.

## If image is too luxurious
Append:
> Reduce polish and advertising gloss. Make surfaces more ordinary, used, matte and observational, with softer daylight and less dramatic contrast.

## If image looks like generic AI abstraction
Append:
> Increase physical material credibility. Make the image feel photographed from paper, glass, dust, metal or linen rather than rendered as a free-floating digital form.

## If image becomes too scientific
Append:
> Remove diagrammatic clarity, axes, labels and explanatory geometry. Preserve evidence and disciplined structure but make interpretation ambiguous.

## If image is too decorative
Append:
> Reduce the number of visual events by half. Increase empty space and let one unresolved material behavior carry the composition.

## If image is too strange
Append:
> Keep all materials physically plausible and familiar. The uncertainty should come from relationship, crop, trace or alignment, not fantasy objects.

## If the one-wrong-thing is too obvious
Append:
> Make the anomaly smaller and less central. It should be discovered after initial viewing, not act as the first focal point.

## If typography has nowhere to sit
Append:
> Reserve at least one uninterrupted calm zone covering roughly one third of the frame, with low local contrast and no important details.
