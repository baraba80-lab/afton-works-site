# AFTON Venture Architecture and Roadmap

Date: 2026-09-13  
Status: strategic design v0.1 / commercial validation required

## One system, five public roles

The projects should not compete as unrelated brands. Each has a distinct economic role.

| Layer | Role | Primary retained value |
|---|---|---|
| Hinge & Rule | founder studio and proof of build capability | trust, cash flow, case studies, physical execution network |
| UNSEEN_RATIO | sensory/editorial research archive | attention, language, cultural context, origin narrative |
| FORMUTE | B2B scent, spatial scent, art/cultural collaboration | paid pilots, partner access, professional demand validation |
| AFTON | consumer product and workshop experience with an explicitly defined operating/ownership structure | brand equity, direct customers, repeat purchase, scent passports |
| FMOC2 / Mixer OS | protected research and operating infrastructure | structured data, decision support, ODM/API/licensing potential |

Soappuccino is a core perfumery and R&D collaborator inside this system. The founder leads concept, planning, structure, design, systems, and marketing; Soappuccino leads perfumery execution, materials, sampling, and sensory judgment. Neither party should become the accidental owner of the other's background assets through ambiguous drafting.

## Economic sequence

The sequence should move from cash-near work to compounding assets without waiting for the final AI system. The objective is not maximum scope; it is maximum useful progress per unit of time and cash.

### Engine 1 — paid design/build proof

Hinge & Rule is ending conventional full-scope interior contracting and its open-ended responsibility. The cash-near offer is one integrated role: a design-capable carpenter paid by the carpentry workday. Measurement, planning, sketching, CAD, material takeoff, order support and direct tool work happen inside that day rather than being sold as separate design or consultation products. Design-only and planning-only commissions are excluded.

Clients directly approve, order and pay for materials. They also directly select, contract, pay and may replace every other trade. Hinge & Rule does not collect the total project budget, direct other trades, manage the whole site or promise overall completion. The objective is not contract avoidance: each workday and furniture delivery still uses a short scope record, and the actual tax/labor/legal classification must be reviewed before a paid pilot. Follow `docs/31_HINGE_FIELD_DESIGN_MAKING_TRANSITION.md` v0.2.

### Engine 2 — FORMUTE B2B pilots

Sell bounded scent-direction engagements for spaces, exhibitions, artists, and small brands. Each engagement must include paid discovery, a defined number of sample iterations, decision documentation, and agreed data/reference rights.

### Engine 3 — AFTON founding product

Launch one tightly scoped founding edition rather than a broad catalogue. Its purpose is to prove:

- people understand and desire the premise;
- the sensory language can guide a product choice;
- manufacturing and compliance are manageable;
- customers return with usable perception feedback.

### Engine 4 — studio calibration experience

Introduce the workshop only after repeatable sample preparation and feedback capture exist. Use precomputed scent tracks before the visit; the visit is for smelling, adjustment, blending, and sealing, not endless live computation.

### Engine 5 — system licensing / ODM / API

Do not sell “AI” before the process has enough validated human–material records. License only a bounded, proven capability. The defensible product is the accumulated alignment between material composition, sensory language, human feedback, and correction history.

## 0–30 days: lock ownership and make the first public surface

Deliverables:

1. resolve AFTON/FORMUTE/UNSEEN_RATIO naming, operating responsibility, ownership, licenses, and recovery terms in writing;
2. correct the joint-R&D agreement before signature;
3. complete the reference contact sheet and approve the landing grammar;
4. publish the lightweight AFTON landing with founder/system credit accessible beyond the hero;
5. define one FORMUTE paid-pilot offer and one AFTON founding-product hypothesis;
6. begin a public-safe build log and a private research/data ledger;
7. photograph real evidence: materials, tools, samples, labels, notes, revisions, and the workspace.

Exit gate: a visitor can understand what AFTON is becoming, who is building it, and how to register interest or contact the studio; role and recovery terms are not ambiguous.

## 31–90 days: prove willingness to pay

Deliverables:

1. run a small number of paid FORMUTE pilots with explicit scopes;
2. produce AFTON founding samples and compliance/manufacturing checks;
3. open a limited reservation, deposit, or qualified-interest path only when fulfillment is credible;
4. structure every sample and feedback event in the Mixer OS data model;
5. publish case evidence that connects concept, material, perception, and revision;
6. measure inquiry quality, conversion intent, iteration time, material cost, and repeat interest.

Exit gate: at least one offer has real payment evidence, repeatable delivery steps, known unit economics, and retained customer feedback.

## 3–6 months: turn projects into a repeatable operating system

Deliverables:

1. standardize intake, sensory vocabulary, sample versioning, and feedback capture;
2. separate reusable base modules from client-specific work;
3. launch the first controlled AFTON edition or workshop pilot;
4. introduce Scent Passport v0 as a record, not as an overclaimed recommendation engine;
5. build a case-study library across Hinge & Rule, FORMUTE, and AFTON;
6. decide which work should be internal, partnered, outsourced, or stopped.

Exit gate: delivery does not depend on reconstructing the process from memory, and every completed engagement adds reusable data or distribution.

## 6–12 months: compound the owned assets

Deliverables:

1. repeat the strongest AFTON product/experience with measured improvements;
2. convert successful FORMUTE pilots into recurring or multi-project relationships;
3. validate limited ODM or system licensing with clear field boundaries;
4. formalize manufacturing, safety, labeling, and test partners;
5. establish an owned customer archive and consent model;
6. prepare funding/support applications around proven research and manufacturing needs, not speculative equipment lists.

Exit gate: the business has more than one revenue route, but all routes strengthen the same research, brand, and operating core.

## Website implications

The temporary landing remains light and visually restrained, but the full information architecture should eventually reveal five dimensions:

1. **AFTON** — product and sensory proposition.
2. **RECORDS** — the numbered UNSEEN_RATIO origin archive.
3. **EVIDENCE** — materials, samples, tools, measured revisions.
4. **WORKS** — spatial, artistic, and partner applications.
5. **STUDIO / METHOD** — the founder, collaborators, research boundaries, and contact.

The hero does not need a biography. The site must nonetheless make it impossible for a serious visitor to conclude that AFTON appeared without an originating builder.

## Content operating system

Rotate three content classes:

- **SENSATION** — ambiguous sensory observation and memory.
- **EVIDENCE** — real material, process, sample, code, test, and failure.
- **OBJECT / APPLICATION** — product, space, exhibition, or client use.

UNSEEN_RATIO currently contains mostly SENSATION. Hinge & Rule contains abundant EVIDENCE and APPLICATION. The new system should connect them without making every account identical.

## Joint-R&D agreement blockers

The current draft must not be treated as ready for signature. Strategic conflicts include:

1. Article 7 currently describes AFTON-related brand assets as Soappuccino's pre-existing property. Confirm whether this matches the parties' real prior agreement, the actual creation history, and the intended operating structure; if it does not, correct it before signature.
2. FORMUTE/AFTON “brand operator” ownership is undefined.
3. joint data and AI-training results default to future agreement, creating a deadlock exactly where the long-term value may accumulate.
4. commercial use requires later agreement but no decision mechanism, license fallback, or exit license is defined.
5. the stated execution date, ten-year term, and 100-day cure period require factual and commercial review.
6. repository/code/data access, model-training rights, formula use, case-study rights, customer ownership, derivative models, and post-termination continuity need explicit schedules.

Before signature, replace ambiguous ownership language with an asset-by-asset owner/license/recovery matrix. Obtain Korean legal review for enforceability and regulatory fit.

## Non-negotiable stop conditions

- no transfer of the AFTON name, domain, visual system, customer data, formula rights, or either party's background method by implication;
- no unpaid open-ended R&D;
- no single-party bottleneck over formulas, samples, manufacturing access, systems, or operational records required for the agreed work to continue;
- no public narrative that erases the founder's actual contribution;
- no heavy site or AI build before a paid offer and data-capture loop are operational;
- no new initiative that cannot state its retained cash, IP, data, proof, audience, or distribution value.
